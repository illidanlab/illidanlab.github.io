01 / Our research

A connected program, 2021–2026

Better molecular design starts
with connected evidence.

A molecule has a structure, many competing properties, and a response that depends on its biological context. Our work connects these pieces: learning useful representations, generating candidates, planning refinements, and testing biological hypotheses with collaborators.

Cell · 2026Biology-guided discovery

Design with the
disease in view.

What if we searched for molecules by the disease-related gene expression changes we want to reverse?

Our collaborative GPS study predicts transcriptional responses from chemical structures, screens compounds, and optimizes leads. Experimental studies in liver cancer and pulmonary fibrosis connect computational design to biological evidence.

The AI building blocks

Complementary methods for different discovery questions.

01MoCL · KDD 2021

Learn molecular structure
with chemical knowledge.

MoCL uses knowledge-aware contrastive learning to build molecular representations. Chemically informed graph transformations and similarities help models learn from unlabeled molecules.

Useful when: property labels are limited.

MoCL paper ↗
02MolSearch · KDD 2022

Explore the trade-offs
between molecular properties.

MolSearch starts with existing molecules and searches through chemical transformations to optimize multiple objectives. Efficient search makes broader exploration practical.

Useful when: a lead must satisfy several goals.

MolSearch paper ↗

Best Paper Award · KDD Health Day Track

03CoG · NeurIPS 2026, accepted
Scaffold→Groups→Constraints

Translate a design brief
into progressive generation.

Chain-of-Generation breaks a text description into ordered semantic goals, guiding molecular diffusion in stages so complex requirements can be introduced progressively.

Useful when: structural intent matters.

Chain-of-Generation paper ↗
04TRACE · KDD 2026

Plan which optimization
tool to use next.

TRACE treats tool selection as a sequence of decisions. An LLM-based agent considers how each refinement affects later steps while retaining similarity to the original lead.

Useful when: good local edits need a longer plan.

Agentic tool planning paper ↗

Together, these studies connect molecular knowledge → controlled design → sequential refinement → biological evaluation. They are complementary research methods, with opportunities to integrate them in new collaborations.

Much of this program grew from a long-standing collaboration with the Bin Chen Lab at Michigan State University, whose expertise in transcriptomics-based drug discovery connects our methods to biology and experimental validation.

02 / The next direction

An AI–experiment collaboration vision

Learn from the next experiment.
