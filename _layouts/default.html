Multimodal Foundation
Models + Agentic AI

We advance AI to tackle critical real-world challenges
with a special emphasis on health informatics, building
tools for healthy aging at home.

Measure: Low-burden signals at home

Detecting the earliest signs of cognitive decline shouldn't require a clinic visit. We develop multimodal digital biomarkers that capture subtle changes in language, speech patterns, and everyday behavior — all from the comfort of home. By combining natural language processing with conversational AI, we're building tools that can screen for early dementia through ordinary interactions, making early detection accessible, low-burden, and scalable to millions.

Validate: Clinical grounding across modalities

Digital signals are only meaningful when they connect to clinical reality. We bridge this gap by grounding our models in federated electronic health records, brain imaging, fluid biomarkers, and clinical outcomes. Through cross-modal learning — linking speech markers to neuroimaging, blood-based indicators, and longitudinal clinical data — we ensure that our AI-driven predictions are not just statistically sound but clinically validated and actionable.

Support: Human-centered, scalable care

Technology should empower, not replace, the human connections at the heart of healthcare. We build AI systems that support continuous screening and monitoring through digital biomarkers, provide conversational AI companions for cognitive health maintenance, and keep humans in the loop at every critical decision point. Our approach emphasizes privacy-preserving methods and equitable access, ensuring these tools can reach the communities that need them most.

Translate: Therapeutics and clinical trials

Understanding disease is only the first step — the ultimate goal is better treatments. We develop AI methods for drug discovery and molecule generation, using deep learning to identify promising therapeutic candidates from transcriptional data. Our work extends to clinical trial enrichment and emulation, enabling researchers to design more efficient trials and accelerate the path from bench to bedside. We leverage agentic AI systems to optimize lead compounds and navigate the vast chemical space more intelligently.
