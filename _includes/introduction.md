At the Intelligent Data Analytics (**ILLIDAN**) Lab @ [University of Michigan](https://umich.edu/), we advance data science and AI to tackle critical real-world challenges (**AI+X**), with a special emphasis on health informatics. Our research centers on **Unified Knowledge Integration** — harnessing AI to unify insights from large-scale, noisy, multimodal, and heterogeneous datasets, integrating domain knowledge with novel machine learning and generative AI techniques.
